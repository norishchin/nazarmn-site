How selling works

Six steps from first walk-through to closing.

  1. Walk-through

    I see the house, ask about updates, and learn your timeline.

  2. Pricing

    You get recent sales, current competition and a recommended price with the reasoning.

  3. Prep

    A short list of fixes that pay for themselves. I skip the ones that don't.

  4. Photos and launch

    Professional photos, a listing written for your house, and a launch timed for the most showings.

  5. Offers

    I lay out each offer side by side: price, financing, contingencies and risk.

  6. Inspection to closing

    I handle the repair negotiation and keep title, lender and buyer's agent moving.
