Common questions

Where in Minnesota do you work?

Anywhere in the state for investment and commercial property. Most of my work is in the Twin Cities metro and the towns within a couple of hours of it.

What is an off-market deal?

A sale that happens without a public listing. The owner and a buyer are introduced directly, usually through an agent who has done the research and made the call.

What are creative terms?

Anything beyond cash or a standard bank loan. The most common are seller carry and contract for deed, where the seller finances part or all of the price. Here is how contract for deed works.

Do you work with first-time investors?

Yes. We start with what you can put down and what return you need, then look at real buildings against those numbers.

I own a building. Can we talk without listing it?

Yes. I'll give you a value range and walk through your options. Nothing is public unless you decide it should be.
