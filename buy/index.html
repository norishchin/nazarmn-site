How buying works

Seven steps, in order.

  1. Lender and budget

    Get pre-approved so you know your payment and sellers take your offer seriously.

  2. The list

    We agree on must-haves, towns and price range before the first showing.

  3. Showings

    I point out roof and furnace age, signs of water, and anything that will cost you later.

  4. Offer

    Price backed by recent sales, with terms built for how much competition the house has.

  5. Inspection

    We sort the report into real problems and routine maintenance, then ask the seller for what matters.

  6. Appraisal and loan

    I keep the lender, title company and seller's agent on schedule.

  7. Closing

    Final walkthrough, sign, keys. I stay available after.
