About Nazar

I was born in Ukraine and came to Minnesota when I was three. I earned an engineering degree and worked as an engineer for about five years before going full time in real estate.

That background shows up in how I work. I read the inspection report line by line, underwrite every investment deal, and give you the number plainly. I'm handy too, so I can give you a realistic read on what a repair takes.

I work out of the RE/MAX Results office in Eden Prairie as part of Team Steady. Outside of work, I'm a husband and a dad.
