01 · The problem

You do this a few times in your life. The other side does it every day.

Buying or selling a home, or a building, is the biggest check most people ever write. The listing agent, the seller and the lender all do this weekly. You deserve someone on your side who does too, and who checks the math.

That's my job: find what a property is really worth, what it will really cost you, and how to get the deal done without surprises. Then I tell you plainly, even when the answer is "walk away."

04 · How we'll work

From first call to keys, in four steps.

Same process whether you're buying a starter home or a fourplex.

  1. A short call

    What you want, your timeline and your budget. No pressure and no paperwork.

  2. A written plan

    Price range, towns or property types, and what a "yes" looks like, so we can move fast when it shows up.

  3. The work

    Showings, pricing, offers and negotiation, with the numbers behind every recommendation.

  4. Closing and after

    Inspection, appraisal and closing handled. I'm still your call for contractors and questions after.
